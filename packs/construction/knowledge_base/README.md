# Construction pack — knowledge base

Authored content for the occupational health and safety construction pack. This is the moat: the
hazard-to-provision-to-action knowledge, written by a competent person and selected by the engine.

## Authoring loop (human-in-the-loop)
1. A draft batch of findings is prepared (provisions, general/specific marking, canonical action
   sourced from Approved Code of Practice / guidance, citation).
2. The qualified professional adjudicates — confirms, corrects, adds — against the source text.
3. The settled version is recorded here and becomes part of the pack.
Work one hazard area at a time, in small batches. A partial knowledge base is usable; nothing waits
on completion.

## Depth
Start shallow: one finding, its principal engaged provisions, the general/specific marking, and one
canonical action from the guidance. Deepen later — depth is additive and does not require redoing
earlier work.

## Each finding records
hazard (the thing with potential to cause harm); control_at_issue (the missing/inadequate
mitigation); tags + reason_codes; engaged provisions with legal_status and duty_scope; the canonical
corrective action with its source publication and acop_derived flag; a comment template in the
"observation of hazard leading to outcome, with priority sentiment" form. See ../../docs/DATA_MODEL.md.
