## Finding 1 — Head-injury hazard; head protection not worn
- hazard: head injury from falling or swinging objects, or striking a fixed low structure
- control_at_issue: head protection (personal protective equipment), not in use
- engaged provisions:
  - Personal Protective Equipment at Work Regulations 1992, regulation 4 (provision) and
    regulation 10 (use) — legal_status: law; duty_scope: SPECIFIC
  - Management of Health and Safety at Work Regulations 1999, regulation 3 (risk assessment) —
    legal_status: law; duty_scope: GENERAL
  - Health and Safety at Work etc. Act 1974, section 2 — legal_status: law; duty_scope: GENERAL
- canonical action (sourced from L25 guidance/Approved Code of Practice): reassess the head-injury
  hazard and apply the hierarchy of control; where head protection remains necessary, provide
  suitable head protection, ensure it is worn in the designated area, and confirm compatibility with
  other protective equipment. Reason-code branch: if provided-but-not-worn, action shifts to
  information, instruction, training, supervision rather than provision.
- acop_derived: TO CONFIRM against L25
